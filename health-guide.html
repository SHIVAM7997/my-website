📚 Health Education & Awareness

Explore simple health education topics and learn about healthy habits for everyday life.

🧼

Hand Hygiene

Cleaning hands properly at important times can help reduce the spread of infections.

🥗

Healthy Nutrition

A balanced diet with a variety of nutritious foods supports overall health and wellbeing.

💧

Hydration

Appropriate fluid intake is an important part of healthy daily habits. Individual needs can vary.

🏃

Physical Activity

Regular movement and physical activity can support physical and mental wellbeing.

😴

Healthy Sleep

A regular sleep routine and adequate rest can help support daily functioning and wellbeing.

🧠

Mental Wellbeing

Talking to trusted people and seeking professional support when needed are important parts of wellbeing.

❤️

Heart Health

Healthy lifestyle choices and regular health check-ups can support long-term cardiovascular health.

🩺

When to Seek Help

Severe, sudden, worsening or concerning symptoms should be assessed by an appropriate healthcare professional.

💡 Remember

Health information can help you make informed choices, but online guidance cannot replace an individual medical assessment. If you have a serious health concern, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
